05 How it works

One counterparty.
Start to finish.

Every transaction follows the same five steps, and every contract records the Incoterm and the place at which title passes.

01

Specification

We take the requirement, write it as a technical specification a supplier can quote against, and agree tolerances, packaging, labelling and certification before anyone is approached.

02

Supplier qualification

We identify suppliers, verify their standing and compliance, and obtain comparable quotations. Counterparty screening against sanctions lists is run before any commitment is made.

03

Contract & purchase

FOKORG International contracts as principal. The contract states the Incoterm, typically FOB or CIF at the port of loading, and the place at which title and risk pass.

04

Shipment & documents

Inspection, loading and the full document set: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and health certificates where required.

05

Arrival & delivery

Customs clearance, transit and refrigerated handling in Abidjan through the group’s own companies, to the buyer’s warehouse rather than to the quayside.

09 Governance

A small company,
well governed.

We publish how we are structured and how we operate, because our counterparties and our bankers are entitled to know before they ask.

Structure

Delaware limited liability company, privately held, governed by a written limited liability company agreement rather than by default statutory rules.

Trading scope

Goods are purchased from suppliers and resold to buyers located outside the United States. Goods do not enter or transit the customs territory of the United States.

Holdings

The company holds no equity interest in any other entity. It is a trading and advisory business, not a holding vehicle.

Payments

Dual authorisation on every outgoing payment. No single signatory can move funds alone, at any amount.

Anti-corruption

The United States Foreign Corrupt Practices Act applies to our conduct worldwide. Facilitation payments are prohibited without exception.

Sanctions and AML

Counterparties are screened against OFAC, United Nations, European Union and United Kingdom lists before any commitment is made.

Related parties

Transactions with affiliated companies are priced on arm’s length terms and documented as such.
